I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the Alliance for Social, Political, Ethical, and Cultural Thought (ASPECT) at Virginia Tech.

Carceral Studies & Decolonial Theory

Focused on the "carceral digital turn" and the evolution of prisoner resistance. My research utilizes the American Prison Writing Archive (APWA) to center the lived experiences and "shadow canons" of incarcerated intellectuals, challenging state-sanctioned narratives of technological control.

Critical Sociology & Social Thought

Investigating the intersection of racial capitalism, surveillance, and epistemic authority. I draw on Stuart Hall, Frantz Fanon, and Karl Marx to theorize how carceral technologies—from electronic monitoring to digital communication platforms—reproduce colonial hierarchies and how these systems are contested from within.

“Frantz Fanon’s Influence on Francesc Tosquelles and the Post-Colonial Legacy of Institutional Psychotherapy”
Target: Theory, Culture, and Society (6,000–8,000 words).

A qualitative article investigating the inverse relationship of influence between Fanon and Tosquelles. This research traces how Fanon's organic activist focus and concept of “sociogeny” fed back into the institutional psychotherapy movement.
